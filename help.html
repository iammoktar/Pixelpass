Help & FAQ

Quick answers about using PixelPass Studio.

Is it free?

Yes. You can edit, download and print without an account.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. Editing happens in your browser. For automatic background removal the browser downloads small AI model files from public servers (Google and jsDelivr); your photo is not sent.

Which photo types can I use?

JPG, JPEG, PNG and WebP photos. A clear, front-facing camera photo gives the best result.

Why does background removal need internet?

The AI model files are loaded the first time you use it. Without internet you can still use the manual strength slider.

How do I get the sharpest print?

Use the original camera photo, keep your face large in the frame, export the PDF and print at 100% / Actual size on photo paper.

Can I change my clothes in the photo?

Not yet. Realistic AI clothing replacement needs a backend AI service, which is not connected. This feature is planned in Dress Studio.
